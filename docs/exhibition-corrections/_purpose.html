Each exhibition below has three lists:

  • Corrected: changes made to the old text, written as old → new: typos, names, dates, punctuation, captions and speaker labels.
  • To verify: things left as written but in doubt, or that couldn’t be confirmed. These need a curator’s answer.
  • New wording to check: text with no old counterpart, such as alt text, captions written new, credit lines and gallery lead captions.

Changes made the same way everywhere are described once, in How the exhibitions were moved, and not listed per exhibition: curly quotes and apostrophes, dashes in date ranges, and speaker labels in bold.

To report a mistake, or to answer a To verify item, note the exhibition and the gallery or clip, and send it to the web team. A fix is made in the exhibition’s data and then on the page, so a rebuild keeps it.
