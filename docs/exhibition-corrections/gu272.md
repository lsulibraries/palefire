## 1. Corrected

### House style, applied everywhere (not listed one by one below)
- Straight quotes and apostrophes changed to curly ones: `'` → `’`, `"` → `“ ”`.
- `--` and `-` used as dashes → `—` (em dash). `...` → `. . .` (spaced ellipsis, as in the Mossville exhibition).
- Commas added after introductory words and before “because” or “and” clauses where the sense needs a pause (“Well when” → “Well, when”, “You know it’s” → “You know, it’s”). Every entry except c1 has some of these.
- “Civil Rights” / “Civil Rights Movement” → “civil rights” / “civil rights movement” (lowercase) in titles and transcripts.
- Clip titles in sentence case, with no closing period.
- Speaker labels in bold on their own paragraphs: the full name the first time (“KARLY JOHNSON:”, “MARY WILLIAMS WAGNER:”), the surname after that.
- Tape timestamps removed from transcripts: [36:11] (c11), [18:33] (c14), [36:08] (c15), [02:12] (c18).
- The old pages’ broken paragraph-break characters (`<br />?<br />` in c11, `</br?>` in c23) became normal paragraph breaks.
- “Link to Biography” links dropped. Each narrator’s biography now sits on their entry. On the old site every link but c1’s pointed nowhere, and c3’s opened Maxine Crump’s bio.
- Credit lines: “Name by Interviewer, Year” → “Name, interviewed by Interviewer, Year”. All-caps names are now written normally (“TONY CLAYTON” → “Tony Clayton”).
- Captions: “X being interviewed by Y” → “X is interviewed by Y”. Dates moved before “Photo by”. “LA” → “Louisiana”. “Jennifer Cramer” → “Jennifer A. Cramer” (c7, c22).
