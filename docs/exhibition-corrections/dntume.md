## 2. To verify

- **"Responsible for the IT decision"** (Theresa Robert): kept as written. It likely means the 1984 Louisiana Supreme Court ruling *Save Ourselves v. Louisiana Environmental Control Commission*, about the IT Corporation hazardous waste facility near Burnside. That isn't confirmed, so nothing was added. A short explanation would help visitors.
- **Clara Baudoin, "House of Representatives"**: the old label didn't say whether she served in the House before, during or after the landfill fight. It now reads "Member of the Louisiana House of Representatives".
- **"Flo Gossen"**: the spelling isn't confirmed.
- **Shirley Goldsmith**: the old page gives no place for her, and CLEAN isn't spelled out. Possibly Calcasieu League for Environmental Action Now, but that isn't confirmed.
- **"Willow Springs Landfill"** and **"ocean incinerator"**: kept as written.
- **Interview years and interviewers**: per clip, unknown. Audio credits give only name and place. The old page says the interviews began in 1999 and the photos date from about 2000.
- **"1970–1990"** in the subtitle: from the old title image. The clips themselves don't give dates.
- **Exhibition year 2001**: from "originally created… in 2001". When the "updated presentation" was made is unknown.
- **Book year 2013** (University Press of Mississippi): from general knowledge, not from the old page.
- **Book link**: https://www.upress.state.ms.us/Books/W/Women-Pioneers-of-the-Louisiana-Environmental-Movement still answers, but it wasn't checked that it lands on the book's own page.
- **Contact**: Jennifer A. Cramer's email (jabrah1@lsu.edu) is kept from the old page. Is she still the right contact?
- **Photographer**: "Photography by Gabriella Mills ca. 2000, courtesy of Peggy Frankland" is applied to all the photos. It likely doesn't cover the editorial cartoon, and it may not cover the landfill sign.
- **Editorial cartoon** (Robert entry): signed "Norwood". The newspaper line under the signature is unreadable at this size (possibly *The Daily Advertiser*). The rights are unknown. The label on David's shirt is also unreadable.
- **High-five photo** (Baudoin entry, `picture16.jpg`): the two women aren't named on the old page, so the photo has no caption. Perhaps Baudoin and Flo Gossen.
- **Cover image** (hands holding a globe, `earth_in_hands.jpg`): the full version of the old title art, taken from a hosted stock image the user believes is royalty-free. The source and license weren't confirmed.
- **Photo size**: the photos are small (the largest is 1,450 px; Robert's is 166 × 287), so leads may look soft on large screens. Better scans may be available from Peggy Frankland.
