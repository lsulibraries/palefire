# A Lady in the South: migration corrections

Exhibition: “A Lady in the South”: LSU Home Management Residence, 1935–1945 (T. Harry Williams Center for Oral History with Hill Memorial Library, 2014 listening station).
Old site: https://lib.lsu.edu/sites/all/files/oralhistory/presentations/LADY/main/ (one page: Home, About, History, Oral History, Photos).
New page: `/v2/special/exhibitions/lady-in-the-south`, built by `scripts/palefire_exhibit_lady_in_the_south.php` from `scripts/data/lady-in-the-south.json`.

New structure: two walls of clips, **Dorothy Colvin Howell** (8) and **A.B. Clark** (7), then **History**, **From the Scrapbook** and **About the Exhibition** as stories. The old “Photos” grid is folded into these galleries.

Changes made throughout, not listed one by one below:

- Straight quotes became typographic quotes and apostrophes (’ “ ”).
- Hyphen dates became en-dash ranges (1935-1945 → 1935–1945).
- Sign-offs at the end of transcripts (“- Dorothy Howell”, “– A.B. Clark”) were dropped, because each player has a credit line.
- Speaker labels are in bold, one paragraph per turn, followed by a colon (“**Dorothy Howell:**”).
- Long transcripts are split into paragraphs at natural breaks.
- “Home Management House(s)/Residence(s)” is in lowercase when it is generic (“the home management houses”). It keeps its capitals in names (“the Helen Carter House”) and in the bios’ “the Home Management House”.

## 2. To verify

- **Howell interview year.** It is unknown, so her credit reads “Dorothy Colvin Howell, interviewed by Jennifer Abraham” with no year. Jennifer Abraham is Jennifer A. Cramer’s earlier name, and the old transcripts label her “Jennifer Abraham”.

- **Names on group1.jpg.** Transcribed from handwriting: “Rose Hewes, Bettye Leigh, “Toinette” Henderson, Miss Elizabeth Tucker (housemother) and Ethel Schenck”. Please check the spellings against the scrapbook.
