This page records how the online exhibitions of LSU Libraries Special Collections moved to the new site, and what is still open. Each exhibition has four lists:

  • Changed on the page: how the exhibition is laid out now: galleries, walls, transcriptions, readings and images added.
  • Corrected: changes made to the old text, written as old → new: typos, names, dates and punctuation.
  • To verify: things left as written but in doubt, or that couldn’t be confirmed. These need a curator’s answer.
  • New wording to check: text with no old counterpart, such as alt text, captions written new, credit lines and gallery lead captions.

Changes made the same way everywhere are described once, in How the exhibitions were moved, and not listed per exhibition. The oral history exhibitions have their own page, the Exhibition Migration Review.

To report a mistake, or to answer a To verify item, note the exhibition and the gallery or entry, and send it to the web team. A fix is made in the exhibition’s data and then on the page, so a rebuild keeps it.
