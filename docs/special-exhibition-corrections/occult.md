# Occult Science and Philosophy in the Renaissance: migration corrections

Exhibition: Occult Science and Philosophy in the Renaissance (Hill Online Exhibitions, 2011; shown in Hill Memorial Library in 2010), drawn from the Rare Book, McIlhenny and Rhoades Collections, curated by Michael Taylor.
Old site: the Wayback Machine’s copy of exhibitions.blogs.lib.lsu.edu/?p=1257&all=1. The students’ copy at /occult was not used, because its text has encoding errors.
New page: `/v2/special/exhibitions/occult`, built by `scripts/palefire_exhibit_occult.php` from `scripts/data/occult.json`.

## Changed on the page

- Eleven galleries on one page at first, one entry per book, each with its catalog record. Since 2026-11-20 the eight themed galleries are one wall, The Objects: the 22 books side by side under their eight themes, each opening full screen with its record and commentary (`scripts/palefire_exhibit_occult_wall.php`).
- 40 images from the “Occult Science 4K” folder.
- Links point at the new anchors, and the Index links to each book.
