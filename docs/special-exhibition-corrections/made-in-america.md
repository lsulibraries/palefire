# Made in America: Selected Imprints from 1776–1826: migration corrections

Exhibition: Made in America: Selected Imprints from 1776–1826, LSU Libraries Special Collections’ exhibition for the nation’s 250th anniversary, in Hill Memorial Library from September 8, 2026, through the fall semester. Curated by Leah Wood Jewett, with text by Brandon Layton, John D. Miles and Lauren Coats; scans by Gabe Harrell.
Old site: one announcement page, /260908/madeinamerica (September 8, 2026), with three paragraphs and two object labels; it is the featured item on /exhibitions.
New page: `/v2/special/exhibitions/made-in-america`, built by `scripts/palefire_exhibit_made_in_america.php` from `scripts/data/made-in-america.json`.

## To verify

Left as written:

- Thomas “was among the men under Paul Revere who rode through the countryside on April 15th”: the ride was the night of April 18, 1775.
- Webster, “the man responsible for the first American dictionary in 1783”: 1783 is his speller (*A Grammatical Institute of the English Language*); his first dictionary is 1806.
- Franklin as “the consummate 19th-century man of letters” (he died in 1790).
- Paine “trained as a staymaker (making wire stays for sails)”: a staymaker made the stays of corsets; a ship’s stays are ropes.
- Franklin: “beginning as an accommodationist who favored reconciliation, by the time he returned…” (a comma where a clause is missing).
- The Introduction lead: which newspaper printed the Louisiana Memorial. The scan (`lba00084_0002.tif`) is the page with the Memorial, without its masthead; its other columns report Alexander Hamilton’s family at church in Albany, so it is August 1804.
- The Isaiah Thomas colophon (1788): which book it ends. The photograph’s neighbor shows a volume with call number “HX … Mor” and a Darney’s Circulating Library (Salem) label.
- Which copy of Darby’s map is on display: the catalog has two 1816 issues (G4010 1816 .D37 MCAGE, the announcement’s, and G4011 .S4 1816 D2 MCAGE). `Darby map.tif` matches the announcement’s image; `AMapOfTheStateOfLouisiana.tif` may be the other.
- Call numbers not found in the catalog search: Wilson’s *American Ornithology* (1808–1814), Dorsey’s *Elements of Surgery* (the catalog’s 1813 copy is COHN AEU7316), the Battle of New Orleans print, the Louisiana Memorial newspaper, the *Price-Current*. *The Emporium of Arts and Sciences* (T1 .E4) and the *Transactions of the American Philosophical Society* (Q11 .P6) are given without volume or year beyond what the scans show.
- Bigelow’s call number is the announcement’s (QK99 .B47 v.1 pt.1 McIlhenny); the tulip poplar may be in a later part.
- The suggested reading list (a QR code on both panels) isn’t on the page: the list itself isn’t in the folder.
