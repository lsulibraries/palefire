# John James Audubon in Louisiana: migration corrections

Exhibition: John James Audubon in Louisiana (LSU Libraries digital exhibition, 2021, from *Audubon at Oakley*, 2008, curated by Elaine Smyth).
Old site: the Wayback Machine’s copies of lib.lsu.edu/exhibitions/john-james-audubon and, for the 78 bird and manuscript pages it never captured, the students’ copy at /audubon (nodes 437–520).
New page: `/v2/special/exhibitions/audubon`, built by `scripts/palefire_exhibit_audubon.php` from `scripts/data/audubon.json`, then `palefire_exhibit_audubon_wall.php`, `palefire_exhibit_audubon_manuscripts.php` and `palefire_exhibit_audubon_readings.php`.

## Changed on the page

- Seven galleries on one page, one entry per bird or manuscript. Headings use the 2021 menu names.
- Elephant Folio, Octavo Edition and Drawings and Proofs are walls: the plates side by side at their own proportions, sortable by plate number or name. The object view shows each edition’s sheet at actual size (40 × 27 in and 11 × 7 in).
- The 11 drawing-and-proof pairs are compared with a slider, each drawing aligned over its proof.
- Manuscripts: The Hurricane and The Bear show all four of their pages (the 2021 site showed a close-up), Children’s Warbler shows its whole page, and each has a transcription, page by page.
- Suggested Reading’s list of citations became typeset covers: Selected works (18 books, 5 articles) and Fiction (2 books), each linked to its catalog record or article.
- Black-throated Waxwing’s drawing is the full sheet, in place of the 2021 close-up.
- The pages’ mis-encoded characters were decoded and checked against the pages the Wayback Machine has. No word differs.

## Corrected

- Spacing and punctuation: spaces around “[now” and “vol. 1”; missing periods and commas; “ : ” and “ ;” in the readings.
- “FIeld” → “Field”
- “Sociey” → “Society”
- “Boneparte” → “Bonaparte” (headings); “Prarie” → “Prairie” (headings); “Flamigo” → “Flamingo” (headings: the students’ titles; the 2021 menus had them right)
- Agreed with the editors: six Latin names (Psittacus, Sylvia childrenii, Sylvia roscoe, Haematopus palliatus, Ictinia mississippiensis, Picus querulus), “Susanne Low”, and Heitman’s pages 44–53.

## To verify
