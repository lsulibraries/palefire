# Woven Louisiana History: Acadian Textiles: migration corrections

Exhibition: Woven Louisiana History: Acadian Textiles, LSU Libraries Special Collections: two interview excerpts with makers for LSU’s Acadian Handicraft Project (1942–1962), Gladys Clark and Laura Mae Romero.
Old site: two Drupal pages, /woven/clark (node 50543) and /woven/romero (node 50545), each with one photograph, an MP3 (/sites/all/files/sc/exhibitions/woven/) and its transcript; the exhibitions tile (node 50546) said only “Interviews with participants in the Acadian Handicraft Project.”
New page: `/v2/oralhistory/exhibitions/woven`, with the other oral history exhibitions, since it is two interviews (one of them from the Williams Center); `/v2/special/exhibitions/woven` redirects there (301). Built unpublished by `scripts/palefire_exhibit_woven.php` from `scripts/data/woven.json`.

## Changed on the page

- One gallery, In Their Words, a wall: Gladys Clark and Laura Mae Romero as picture tiles (photograph, clip, transcript and a short biography, opening in the object view), with two text plates: The Acadian Handicraft Project between them, About the Interviews after.
- New text, all from LSU Libraries finding aids or the old pages’ own source lines: the introduction, the two biographies (Gladys Clark’s from the Gladys Clark Papers, Mss. 4953, finding aid, 2009, updated 2016; Laura Mae Romero’s from her own words in the interview), The Acadian Handicraft Project (from the Acadian Handicraft Project Records, Mss. 1880, finding aid, 2014), and About the Interviews (the old pages’ lines under each player).
- The cover is Gladys Clark’s portrait, framed (shown whole over a blur, since it is upright), with an object label: Gladys Clark with handwoven placemats, 1980s, Photograph, Gladys Clark Papers, Mss. 4953.
- Each clip has a title from its own words (“I’m an Acadian weaver”, “I know my work”) and a short credit (speaker, interviewers, year); the fuller citation is in About the Interviews.
- Audio: the old MP3s, converted to AAC (64 kbps mono, −16 LUFS) like the other oral history exhibitions. Clips: Gladys Clark 8:24, Laura Mae Romero 11:02.
- The old Clark page’s “Contact the staff” line pointed to /oralhistory#ohfaq; About the Interviews links to /v2/oralhistory.
