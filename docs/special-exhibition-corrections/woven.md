# Woven Louisiana History: Acadian Textiles: migration corrections

Exhibition: Woven Louisiana History: Acadian Textiles, LSU Libraries Special Collections: two interview excerpts with makers for LSU’s Acadian Handicraft Project (1942–1962), Gladys Clark and Laura Mae Romero.
Old site: two Drupal pages, /woven/clark (node 50543) and /woven/romero (node 50545), each with one photograph, an MP3 (/sites/all/files/sc/exhibitions/woven/) and its transcript; the exhibitions tile (node 50546) said only “Interviews with participants in the Acadian Handicraft Project.”
New page: `/v2/oralhistory/exhibitions/woven`, with the other oral history exhibitions, since it is two interviews (one of them from the Williams Center); `/v2/special/exhibitions/woven` redirects there (301). Built unpublished by `scripts/palefire_exhibit_woven.php` from `scripts/data/woven.json`; `scripts/palefire_exhibit_woven_press.php` brings a page built before the press release changes up to date.
Press release: [“Woven Louisiana History: Acadian Textiles” Exhibition Opens In September](https://lib.lsu.edu/24/08/29/fallexhibition), LSU Libraries, August 29, 2024. The online pages were part of the exhibition shown in Hill Memorial Library, September 23–December 13, 2024, with textiles from the LSU Textile & Costume Museum.
