# New from Old Natchez: migration corrections

Exhibition: New from Old Natchez, manuscripts about the Natchez area recently acquired by LSU Libraries Special Collections, shown at the Historic Natchez Foundation from February to August 2002 during the Historic Natchez Conference. Texts by Tara Zachary, Assistant Curator for Manuscripts.
Old site: the Showcase pages /natchez/introduction (node 263) and nodes 290–298 (eight collections and the bibliography), whose “Image” and “Transcript” links go to the 2002 files under /sites/all/files/sc/exhibitions/e-exhibits/natchez/collections/. The text is taken from the Showcase pages, the transcriptions from the 2002 files.
New page: `/v2/special/exhibitions/natchez`, built unpublished by `scripts/palefire_exhibit_natchez.php` from `scripts/data/natchez.json`.

## Changed on the page

- Ten galleries, every one a wall with a lead picture: Introduction; the eight collections (Conner, Farrar, Hunt, McMurran-Austen, Muggah-Glover-Guyther, Peirce-Haralson-Rumble, Schwartz, Stewart); About the Exhibition.
- Each collection opens with text plates (the family or person, and the papers), each with a Summary taken from its own sentences. The Schwartz note on the Federal occupation, two long paragraphs under one ledger, is its own plate, Natchez under occupation.
- Each numbered item under “Notes on the images” is an entry: its pages (turned in the object view, “4 pages” on the wall), its note, and the transcription where the old site had one, under a Transcription heading. The old “Image 1.1–1.4 | Transcript” links are gone.
- Peirce items “1 and 2” (two letters from Rebecca Minor) are two entries; each keeps the shared note and the sentence on the planters’ travels.
- Introduction: the opening quote and the paragraphs on Alie McMurran and on the exhibition are text plates; its three pictures hang as tiles (the Shenak letter, the baptism and La Belle Talton). The paragraph on the Louisiana and Lower Mississippi Valley Collections and the credits move to About the Exhibition, with the bibliography.
- Removed: the 2002 contact details (Tara Zachary’s e-mail and phone, the Historic Natchez Foundation’s address and phone, “Contact them for more information about the physical exhibit”). The physical exhibition is in the credit line, the physical-exhibition note and the Credits plate.
- Cover: the 1913 baptism photograph from the Conner scrapbook, with an object label. It is a 1244×914 crop of a small print on a 300 dpi page scan, under 720p, and no larger copy exists, so “Frame the cover” is ticked: it shows whole at its own size over a blurred wash of itself.
- Gallery leads: all ten are 1,500px or more (the smallest is Alie McMurran’s letter, 1500×2317), so none has “Show whole image” and each fills the screen.
- Titles: each entry has a short title (“Israel Loring to David Hunt, 1817”); the old pages had only numbers.
